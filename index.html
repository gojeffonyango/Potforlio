Welcome! I’m a geoscientist working at the intersection of geochemistry, hydrogeology, and applied exploration geophysics. My research focuses on the East African Rift Basalts and Proterozoic ophiolites, with particular emphasis on CO₂ mineralization in the Kenya Rift, and geological hydrogen in ophiolitic systems. I use numerical modelling and simulation to investigate these geological processes and their potential applications in climate resilience, carbon management, and clean energy.

Currently developing a formal reactive transport modeling framework — from 0-D kinetic batch simulations through 2-D transport — for CO₂–basalt interaction in the Kenya Rift.

Beyond my research work, I consult on applied geoscience projects across Africa, including geophysical mineral exploration surveys and hydrogeological modeling, and I'm interested in bridging rigorous geochemical modeling with practical decision-making for carbon storage and resource exploration in the region.
